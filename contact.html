Customer Support

We understand that customers may have different furniture needs. Some customers may be furnishing a new home, while others may be replacing or updating furniture in an existing home.

Our website provides information about our organisation and furniture categories so that customers can make enquiries based on their individual requirements.

Visit or Contact Us

Customers can use our contact information to communicate with Xichavo Furniture & Décor.

For product-related questions, you can also visit our Enquiry page and complete the online enquiry form.

Frequently Asked Questions

What type of furniture do you offer?

Our website focuses on furniture for bedrooms, living rooms, sofas, couches and other areas of the home.

Can I ask about a product before purchasing?

Yes. Customers can use the enquiry page to request information about products.

Who is the website designed for?

The website is aimed at individuals furnishing new homes, families looking for affordable furniture, young adults setting up their first homes and customers searching for household furniture.

How can I make an enquiry?

Visit the Enquiry page, complete the form and provide details about the product you are interested in.
